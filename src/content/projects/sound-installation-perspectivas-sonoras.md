<p>Perspectivas Sonoras is a sound installation created as part of the Multimedia Installation Workshop at Berklee College of Music. The main idea is to create a visual and sonic perception dissonance caused by the interaction of resonant bodies in a closed space. The sound is based in a series of slowly evolving oscillators programmed in the Max/MSP environment and played through surface speakers attached to several resonant bodies.</p>
<p>The installation was presented during july 7 and 8, 2017, at Museu de les Ciències Príncipe Felipe in Valencia, Spain.</p>
