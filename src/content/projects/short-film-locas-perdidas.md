<p>In 1996, Rodrigo (18) goes back home after being arrested by the police in a televised raid in the club where he worked as a drag queen, fearful that his family will see him on the news. While them get ready for a party, he plans to run away with his couple, a hairdresser who is a friend of the family.</p>
